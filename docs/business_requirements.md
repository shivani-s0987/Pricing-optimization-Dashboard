# Business Requirements

**Problem:** Retail pricing and category teams need a consistent view of revenue, gross profit, margins and discounting to identify products for investigation. **Users:** Pricing analyst, category manager, BI analyst, finance partner. **Decisions supported:** prioritize product-level reviews, compare discount bands descriptively, inspect regional/channel mix, and test explicit price/demand/cost scenarios. **Not in scope:** automatic price deployment, validated market pricing, causal elasticity, net profit, or real-company forecasts.

Success criteria: reproducible 20k+ line dataset; explicit data-quality quarantine; consistent metric definitions; SQL analytical views/queries; formula-driven Excel workbook; Power BI source implementation artifacts; automated CSV/Markdown/PDF reports; testable reconciliations; transparent synthetic-data caveat.
